ARTEMIS · design of record · five-county north-Florida pilot

FVS leaves the scheduling loop

ARTEMIS builds a library of candidate trajectories for every stand, where the stand's ownership class decides which prescriptions are eligible. FVS runs once per (stand, prescription), offline, with no restart barriers. A simulated-annealing scheduler then picks one trajectory per stand. Simulation enumerates what each stand could do; the scheduler decides what it will do.

Why this architecture 01

Precomputing trajectories turns scheduling into arithmetic

The previous design ran every stand to a 5-year barrier, solved an allocation, injected cuts and resumed. Each candidate plan cost a full FVS re-projection, so only one greedy forward pass was affordable.

Iterative coupling (retired)Trajectory library + annealing (adopted)
Cost of evaluating one planA full FVS re-projectionA table lookup and a sum
Plans the scheduler can considerOne greedy forward passMillions
FVS barriers per runn_cycles per standZero — one continuous run
ParallelismSynchronised at every barrierEmbarrassingly parallel over (stand, prescription)
Decision spaceImplicit, discovered as the run proceedsExplicit, enumerated up front, auditable

A second consequence carbon

The FFE carbon corruption — Forest_Shrub_Herb collapsing to 0.02 and stand carbon ~8% low at every barrier — is a stop/restart artifact. Library runs have no barriers, so it does not arise. carbon_extension stays false as a scope decision, not a defect.

What it costs frozen decision space

A prescription that was not enumerated cannot be selected. State-dependent silviculture ("thin when SDI crosses 450") must be FVS event-monitor logic inside a trajectory, enumerated as its own prescription — never recovered in the scheduler.

No precedent to lean on 05

Five choices are ARTEMIS's own, and each needs its own defence

Ownership keys the library config

No reference keys a prescription library to a national 30 m, seven-class ownership raster across a mixed-ownership landscape.

Imputed lists composed to units methods

TreeMap → FIA plots → area-weighted union per unit. Diaz et al. used GNN imputation; this composition rule is ours to defend.

State BMP geometry as the absolute riparian

Their exclusions were federal designations; ours are Florida stream-management zones from hydrography.

Targets dimensioned by county × owner against a known artifact

Diaz et al. set one global target and their scheduler shifted harvest between BLM Districts to hit it. Keeping dimensions is chosen specifically to avoid that.

Barrier-free runs, by measurement restart fidelity

The restart-fidelity arms are why library trajectories can be asserted clean where coupled runs were not.

Vocabulary 06

The 693 are FIA plots, not stands

FIA plot PLT_CN

An inventory sample with a measured tree list. TreeMap imputes one per 30 m pixel. 693 unique in the pilot, median footprint ~107 ac. Data, not a place.

Management unit MU_ID

A delineated decision polygon — the modelling "stand". Union County: 17,020 raw polygons → 2,442 after sliver-merge. Initialised from the area-weighted mix of its plots.

FVS stand STAND_ID

The simulation unit. = plot in the no-management baseline (stand_cn == PLT_CN); = management unit in the target. The 2026-08-31 "stands" are pixel classes, a third, interim thing.

Ownership decides the menu, not the meal 07

Three rules turn an owner class into a library

1 · no_management is always there

Otherwise a binding volume target has no feasible answer, and "the plan harvested this stand" stops being a decision. In 2026-08-31 the scheduler left 116k upland acres unmanaged by choice.

2 · Riparian is structural

SMZ_Pct ≥ 50 → a library of exactly {no_management}. No objective weight can trade no-entry away, because there is no alternative to select.

3 · Screens only remove

Minimum harvest age, reserve status and operability drop prescriptions at build time. A library screened to {no_management} is valid and must be logged.

One library per stand 08

Stand = management unit, grown from the weighted union of its plots

Weighted union, not an average build_tree_init

Every donor tree is kept intact; its TPA is scaled by its plot's area share of the unit (LETO 5% floor, then renormalise). The diameter distribution and species mix survive, and one FVS run per unit needs no re-aggregation.

Modelling assumption: FVS computes mortality over the pooled list, so trees from plot A compete with trees from plot B as if co-located. State it in the methods.

Dedup is a cache never a reporting decision

Key the FVS run cache on a hash of (tree list, site attributes, prescription). The library still carries one row set per (stand, prescription), and every polygon keeps its identity — the 2026-08-31 plan carries trajectory_id (11,831 distinct) and the shared fvs_run_id (2,482) side by side.

Budget: target 6–12 trajectories per stand, about 10⁵ FVS runs for the pilot. Growth is multiplicative, so grid resolution is a standing budget question.

trajectory_cycles joined after selection

One row per (trajectory_id, cycle): calendar year, age, BA, TPA, QMD, SDI, total and merchantable ft³, board feet, removals, RmvCode, and the five carbon pools when enabled.

Harvest years: FVS writes two rows. Take removals from the RmvCode = 1 row and state from the post-cut row — collapsing by largest removal keeps the pre-cut state.

Every trajectory has exactly n_cycles + 1 state rows: the inventory plus one endpoint per cycle.

  • TPO figures are a target, not a ceiling. They derive from observed removals, so undershooting a county is as much a finding as overshooting it.

The scheduler · search 12

Metropolis acceptance, geometric cooling, a calibrated start

Moves mixture

Single-stand (0.70) reassigns one stand. Block (0.20) reassigns an adjacency block — single moves stall under green-up. Period swap (0.10) trades timing between comparable stands.

Without adjacency the block move is dropped and the rest renormalise to 0.875 / 0.125.

Temperature calibrated

Accept a worsening move of size Δ with probability exp(−Δ/T). Solve T₀ so 80% of sampled worsening moves are accepted, then T ← 0.95·T, 10 iterations per stand per level, stop at T < 1e-4 or 20 levels without improvement.

Reproducible seeded

Seed from the greedy allocator. Seed 42, five restarts, every seed reported. Same seed + library + weights ⇒ the same plan.

Validation 16

Growth first, then the library, then the plan

Growth planned

Initialise from an older FIA panel, project, compare against the latest remeasurement: bias and RMSE for BA, TPA, QMD, volume by species group. Cross-check year-0 biomass against BIGMAP; county totals against EVALIDator; probe ±10% site index.

Library integrity every build

Riparian → exactly no_management. Others ≥ 2 unless screened down, and logged. n_cycles + 1 rows, no NaNs. Unit layer and library cover each other both ways. Every prescription is in its owner's menu.

Plan plausibility headline results

The chosen prescription mix by owner class (Diaz et al. Fig. 16), per-county outcomes, harvested area against TPO and LCMS, age classes through time, opening sizes against green-up.

The fitted LCMS harvest model changes job. The optimiser now generates the schedule, so P(harvest | features) becomes calibration evidence: observed harvest rates by owner, county and age are what the plan is checked against. Disagreement localises to the menu or the weights — both inspectable.

In one slide 20

The architecture, and what it has proven so far

  • FVS leaves the loop — enumerate each stand's eligible futures once, then select among them; a whole plan costs a lookup and a sum.
  • Ownership defines the menu — no_management always present, riparian structurally {no_management}, screens only remove.
  • The first plan is real — 11,831 stands, 3,781 FVS runs, objective 190.41 against greedy 363.68, converged across five seeds, riparian no-entry intact.
  • The decision space is the limit, not the search — 47 of 80 targets are provably out of reach, because the library has no timing variants.
  • Half the constraints are dark — stands are still pixel classes, so adjacency, green-up and opening size wait on the unit × stand crosswalk.
The honest limit: simulated annealing carries no optimality guarantee. A plan is a result only with its violation vector, bound, baselines and seed spread — and nothing yet validates FVS growth against FIA remeasurement.
