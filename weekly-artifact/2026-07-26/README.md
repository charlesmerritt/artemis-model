## What the figures say

**The even-flow target is real and quantified (fig 1, 2).** USFS TPO reports give
16 observation years for the five counties. Total harvest averages **72.1 M ft³/yr**
across all years and **77.5 M ft³/yr** over 2013–2024, split 92% private /
5.5% other public / 2.5% federal. Annual swings are large — CV 14% for private, 36%
for other public, 46% for federal, and 21–36% by county. That dispersion is
exactly what a non-declining-flow constraint is meant to remove, and the 2013–2024
means are the natural first targets for the rolling-horizon LP.

**Supply comfortably covers demand now, and stops doing so around 2041 (fig 3).**
Expanding the no-management FVS run over its 1,204,028 painted acres, standing
merchantable volume grows from **3.0 to 6.6 billion ft³** over 2026–2076. But
annual net growth (accretion − mortality) *falls* from 133 M ft³/yr to 24 M ft³/yr
as the stands age past their fast phase. It crosses below the 77.5 M ft³/yr
observed harvest level around **2041**. Under a no-management trajectory the AOI
cannot sustain the historical harvest indefinitely — which is the argument for the
scheduler rather than against it: harvesting resets stands into their high-growth
years, and closing that loop is what the even-flow allocation does. The figure
charts the unharvested trajectory only, so read the crossing as a bound on the
naive case, not a forecast.

**The bundling rule as specced does not survive contact with the data (fig 7).**
`2026-07-17-orchestrator-sketch.md` proposes assigning each stand to its plurality
ownership class when that class clears ~70% of the stand's pixels. Tabulating
Harris 2025 ownership over each stand's actual footprint: at 70% only **364 of 693
stands** qualify, holding **40% of the acreage**. The cause is structural, not a
tuning problem — a TreeMap "stand" is an imputed FIA plot painted onto many
scattered pixels, so its footprint straddles owners by construction. Even a 50%
threshold drops 61 stands. **Pixel-share allocation** (each stand contributes its
acres to each owner proportionally: 830k private / 151k federal / 41k other public)
keeps the whole AOI and is the workable alternative, at the cost of a stand
belonging to more than one bundle. The clean fix is to bundle at the management-unit
level (`pipeline/s3_management`) rather than the imputed-plot level; until those
units exist, pixel-share is what the even-flow solve should use.

**The barrier is doing real work (fig 4).** The 693 stands enter at 24 distinct
inventory years spanning 1997–2021, and only from 2026 does every stand report on a
shared 5-year cycle. Within a process FVS finishes one stand before starting the
next, so stands are never simultaneously visible at a common year — the stop/restart
barrier is what makes a bundle-wide allocation at year *t* possible at all.

**The mechanism question is closed (fig 8).** The 30% thin removed exactly 30% of
both TPA and BA (retained fraction 0.700 on each), and all three injection
mechanisms — native `ThinDBH` keyword, `fvsCutNow` in-process, and `fvsAddActivity`
after a stop/restart — agree to `max |Δ| = 0.0` on every summary metric. Per-stand
targeting inside a bundle works too: the non-target stand is bit-identical to its
no-cut baseline.

The ownership raster is never downloaded whole. It is a 249,593 × 580,440
EPSG:4269 tiled GeoTIFF; the script opens it through GDAL's `/vsis3` driver and
warps it onto the 5-county TreeMap grid with a `WarpedVRT`, so only the tiles the
AOI window touches move over the wire (~35 s). The warped 3,649 × 4,539 array is
cached as `ownership_aoi_5070.npy`.

Inputs land in `data/interim/r2_cache/` (gitignored) and are reused on re-runs;
delete the folder to force a refetch. **No input data is committed.**

## Method notes

- **Stand → pixel join** is the same `TM_ID → PLT_CN → stand_cn` path
  `pipeline/s4_fvs/paint_fvs_to_raster.py` uses, on the TreeMap 2022 vintage
  (the 2022-vs-2020 trap in `notes/fvs-to-raster-painting.md` applies here too).
  The script asserts ≥99% of AOI pixels join before computing anything;
  5,413,921 / 5,414,572 do.
- **Acres** are pixel counts × 900 m² ÷ 4046.8564224. AOI total: 1,204,028 ac.
  FVS `sampling_weight` is *not* used for expansion — those are FIA plot expansion
  factors over a much larger population (they sum to 3.8 M ac), not AOI acres.
- **Harris → TPO owner groups**: family + corporate + tribal → Private,
  state + local → Other public, federal → Federal (NF). Harris classes
  non-forest / water / unknown-forest fall inside painted stands for 15% of AOI
  acres; fig 3 keeps them as an unattributed grey band rather than dropping them,
  so the stack and the growth panel describe the same footprint.
- **Fig 8 is not R2 data.** It charts the measured values recorded in
  `research/restart_fidelity/outputs/gate_cut_injection.txt`; the spike's own FVS
  outputs were never uploaded to R2.

## Dependencies / environment

`requires-python = ">=3.14"`. The figures use `boto3`, `numpy`, `pandas`,
`rasterio` and `matplotlib` — nothing 3.14-specific — so a 3.13 environment works
if 3.14 is unavailable:

```bash
uv venv --python 3.13 .venv && uv pip install boto3 numpy pandas rasterio matplotlib openpyxl
```

Versions used: numpy 2.5.1, pandas 3.0.3, rasterio 1.5.0, matplotlib 3.11.1,
boto3 1.43.56, openpyxl 3.1.5. `boto3` and `openpyxl` are not yet in
`pyproject.toml` dependencies; add them if this script becomes part of the
pipeline rather than a weekly artifact.
