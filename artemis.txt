Notes on what changed, and why
------------------------------

FVS LEAVES THE SCHEDULING LOOP. The earlier design ran every stand to a 5-year
restart barrier, gathered state, solved an allocation, injected cuts, and resumed.
That made each candidate plan cost a full FVS re-projection, so the scheduler could
only afford a single greedy forward pass. Precomputing the trajectories converts
scheduling into selection over a fixed discrete set, and a metaheuristic becomes
worth using.

A SECOND CONSEQUENCE: the FFE carbon corruption measured in
notes/restart-fidelity-findings.md is a stop/restart artifact. Library trajectories
have no barriers, so it does not arise. carbon_extension stays false and its tripwire
test stays in place, but the blocker is now a scope decision, not a defect.

WHAT THIS COSTS: the decision space is frozen when the library is built. A
prescription that was not enumerated cannot be selected. State-dependent silviculture
("thin when SDI crosses 450") must be expressed as FVS event-monitor logic INSIDE a
trajectory, enumerated as its own prescription -- not recovered in the scheduler.

CLIMATE SCENARIO is not an axis of the library in v1 (PLAN.md scope notes place
climate-modified growth out of scope). It is a natural fourth axis if that changes;
reading the Climate-FVS reference should settle it.
