Baker, Columbia, Hamilton and Union are within 2% of their targets in every period, slightly over in fact, which is the annealer leaning on them to make up some of the landscape shortfall. Suwannee is the miss: −30% on average, about −37% through the 2040s, closing to −9% by 2072. Suwannee has the largest TPO target of the five, 112 million cubic feet a period, and the smallest forest supply relative to it.
