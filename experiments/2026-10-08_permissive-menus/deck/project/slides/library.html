Opening the menus is cheap in FVS terms, because prescriptions are shared: a plot runs a regime once, however many owners might choose it. The library goes from 4.5 options per stand to 17.8, and from about 12,500 distinct FVS runs to about 25,800. The runs take about 20 minutes on 11 cores. Two ingredients besides the open menus: offsets now go out to 25 years, and rotations repeat. The repeat matters more than it sounds. In the last deck each keyfile cut a stand at most once in the 50 years. Here a 25-year pine rotation clearcuts, replants through FVS's establishment model, and can be thinned and cut again within the horizon. One FVS run out of 32,850 failed; that single option is dropped and every stand keeps a full menu.
