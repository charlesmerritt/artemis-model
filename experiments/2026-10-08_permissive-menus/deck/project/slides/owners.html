The annealer scores each TPO owner group separately, because owner classes harvest consistently in aggregate. Bars are planned removals per period, the orange line is that group's TPO target. Federal, essentially Osceola National Forest, is on target in every period (mean −0.1%). Private, which carries 92% of the target, averages −7.8%, closing from about −10% early to −2% by 2072. Other public, state and local land, averages −14.1%. That group's target is 22 million cubic feet a period from about 34,000 acres, and even with every stand on its highest-volume option it can supply only 96% of ten periods of target, so the early periods cannot be filled.
