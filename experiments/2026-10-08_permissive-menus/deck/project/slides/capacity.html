Suwannee: 76% at most

Even with every stand on its highest-volume option, the county cannot reach its 50-year TPO total. No schedule from this library can.

Upper bound: every stand takes its largest 50-year removal, independently · horizon_capacity.py

Is the remaining miss the search, or the forest? This is a hard upper bound: for each target, put every stand on whichever option removes the most over 50 years, ignoring even flow entirely. If that cannot reach ten periods of target, no schedule can. Suwannee tops out at 76% of its 50-year TPO total. The plan delivers 70%. Other public tops out at 96% and the plan delivers 86%, and the plan cannot hit early periods because so much of that capacity is in stands that only become harvestable later. Every other target has room to spare: Federal could supply seven times its target, which is why the annealer uses it so lightly. So the residual error is a supply statement about this library on this landscape. Part of it may be real, Suwannee's TPO removals may draw on timber from outside the county or from stands the TreeMap classes underrepresent, and part is the pilot's limits, since these stands are pixel classes on the published TreeMap rather than LETO units on the improved rasters.
