Total error, three ways, against the owner-specific menus from the last deck. Both columns use the same 11,831 stands, the same TPO targets, the same objective and the same annealer; only the menus differ, so every gap is the menu. The even-flow cost is what the annealer actually minimises: the sum, over 5 counties and 3 owner groups in each of 10 periods, of the squared relative miss. It falls from 10.5 to 1.28. In plain percentages, the average owner group misses its target by 7.4% in a period, down from 29.5%, and the average county by 6.8%, down from 31%. The five-county total averages −7.9%. The worst single cell is Suwannee in the 2040s at −37%. Where the reduction came from, from the October 8 sweep: opening every regime to every owner alone takes the cost from 10.5 to 5.6; letting rotations repeat inside the horizon takes it to 1.4; offsets out to 25 years take it to 1.28. Ignoring forest type entirely would only reach 1.23, so keeping forest type costs almost nothing.
