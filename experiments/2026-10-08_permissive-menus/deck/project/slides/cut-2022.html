The cut-by-cut step-through starts here: the five counties in 2022, shaded by standing merchantable volume per acre, from the same FVS runs the schedule uses. Over the next ten slides each period's cuts are painted on: dark red for clearcuts, which FVS replants in the same keyfile, and amber for thins and selection cuts. Green is what stands between cuts. A caveat on reading the maps: the plan's stands are pixel classes, TreeMap plot by county by owner, not contiguous management units, so one decision paints scattered pixels across a county. Riparian buffers are masked and never shown cut.
