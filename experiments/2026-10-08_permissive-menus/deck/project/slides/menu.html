config/management_regimes.yaml · forest type still filters: no pine plantation on a hardwood stand

Here is the change. Rows are owner classes, columns are the eight cutting prescriptions. Dark dots are what each class could do in the last deck, two or three options each. Light dots are what this run opens up. Every class now has all eight. So family forest can clearcut and replant pine on a short rotation, corporate land can do a light thin or a selection cut, and federal and state land can run either rotation. Forest type is the one thing still held fixed, along the bottom: the two pine plantation rotations only go on pine stands, the hardwood clearcut only on hardwood or mixed stands, the rest on anything. The stands, their tree lists and their volumes are identical to the last deck. Two more things are switched on. Short rotations now repeat inside the 50 years: a stand clearcut in 2030 can be thinned and clearcut again before 2072, replanted each time. And every regime can start 0, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25 years late, the Diaz et al. timing offsets, out to the 25 years the design calls for. Riparian buffers are untouched: grow only, never cut.
