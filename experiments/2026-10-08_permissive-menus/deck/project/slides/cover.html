Five-county north Florida pilot: Baker, Columbia, Hamilton, Suwannee, Union · run 2026-10-12

This deck asks one question: what happens to the harvest schedule if we stop assigning management by owner class and let every owner do everything? Every owner class may now take every cutting prescription we have defined: thin, select, clearcut, short pine rotation, long pine rotation, natural-regeneration rotation. Forest type still matters: a pine plantation can only go on a pine stand, a hardwood clearcut only on hardwood or mixed. The ground does not change, only what each owner is allowed to do with it. Simulated annealing then picks one trajectory per stand to match TPO removals by county and by owner group in every five-year period. Headline: the five-county harvest comes in 8% under target on average, against 33% under with the owner-specific menus from the last deck.
